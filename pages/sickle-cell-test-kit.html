Clinical Significance

Sickle cell disease is a genetic blood disorder. Early detection helps in managing the condition and preventing complications.

  • Detects sickle cell anemia
  • Rapid screening tool
  • Supports neonatal screening
Rapid Test

Product Overview

Our Sickle Cell Test Card is a rapid test for the detection of sickle cell anemia in human blood samples.

It provides quick and reliable results, making it suitable for use in various healthcare settings.
